Meet Your Physician

Dr. Muhammad Talha Hassan Shah

MBBS (University of Health Sciences, Lahore) • Registered Physician

Dr. Muhammad Talha Hassan Shah is a dedicated physician trained at the University of Health Sciences, Lahore, with hands-on clinical rotations across Internal Medicine, General Surgery, Anesthesiology, Pediatrics, and Emergency Care at prominent teaching hospitals including Rai Medical College Teaching Hospital, Pakistan Institute of Medical Sciences (PIMS), and Polyclinic Hospital Islamabad.

Committed to a holistic and communicative practice, Dr. Talha bridges evidence-based medicine with clear, empathetic patient counseling. In addition to bedside clinical care, he actively engages in public health literacy, helping patients understand chronic prevention, lifestyle modifications, and early warning signs.
